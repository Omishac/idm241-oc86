Case Study: Poppi Soda Product Card

The Overview

This project focuses on the redesign of the Poppi Soda product card to enhance user engagement and improve the visual representation of the brand's identity. Poppi Soda, known for its vibrant and health-focused beverages, aims to stand out through innovative design and an approachable aesthetic. The redesigned product card prioritizes functionality, interactivity, and an engaging user experience while showcasing key product details. The primary goals of the redesign include capturing the brand's playful essence, streamlining usability, and creating microinteractions that delight and inform users, ultimately driving higher conversion rates.
