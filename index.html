Case Study: Poppi Soda Product Card

The Overview

Poppi Soda, a fast-growing health beverage brand, wanted their online product cards to be as fun and engaging as their drinks. The original cards were simple but didn’t spark much interaction or capture the brand’s playfulness. This project was about bringing life to the card by adding microinteractions, making the shopping experience more enjoyable and true to Poppi’s branding.

Context and Challenge

Background and Description

Poppi Soda, a fast-growing health-beverage brand known for its vibrant cans and gut-health benefits. The project aimed to redesign the product cards on their e-commerce site to better reflect the brand’s playful personality and engage with their customers more on their site. The existing design was functional but lacked interactivity causing lack of potential to capture the spirit of the brand.

Currently, the Poppi Soda product cards lack interactivity. The design of the card is more static which led to low engagement and limited user exploration.

  • Timeline: 10 weeks
  • Budget: Zero Dollars

Purpose: The primary goal of the redesign was to create a more engaging shopping experience with their online site. By enhancing the product cards, Poppi Soda aimed to increase user interaction.

The Problem

Currently, the Poppi Soda product cards lack interactivity. The design of the card is more static which led to low engagement.

Goals & Objectives

This solo project focused on making Poppi Soda’s product card more interactive and engaging. By adding playful microinteractions that reflect the brand’s vibrant, health-conscious personality, the goal was to encourage exploration and increase user engagement.

The metrics that will define if this project is successful are through the following metrics and tangible goals:

  • Increased Engagement: Boost user interactions with the product card by adding microinteractions that are triggered by clicks and hovers.
  • Improved User Experience: Ensure smoother navigation and a more intuitive interface.
  • Mobile Responsiveness: Achieve seamless functionality and design adaptability across devices, ensuring a consistent experience on screens of all sizes.
