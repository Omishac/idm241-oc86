Orange Soda

TRIGGERS:

  • Hover On/Off Buy Now Button
  • Click Buy Now Button
  • Click On/Off Small Soda Icon: Default
  • Click On/Off Small Soda Icon: Active
  • Hover On/Off Soda Label
  • Mouse Down/Up Soda Image
  • Hover On/Off Product Card

RULES:

Buy Now Button

  • Hover On- Cursor changes to finger and button & text color changes
  • Hover Off- Cursor changes back to pointer and button returns to original button color & text
  • Click- User clicks on button and a loading page opens with orange background and purple bubbles floating up before going to the soda's Amazon page opens

Click Small Soda Icon: Default

  • Click On- Soda card shakes and fades into the image of front of can
  • Click Off- Soda card shakes and fades into the image of back of can

Click Small Soda Icon: Active

  • Click On- Soda card shakes and fades into the image of back of can
  • Click Off- Soda card shakes and fades into the image of front of can

Hover On/Off Soda Label

  • Hover On- Cursor changes to finger and product card inverts background gradient
  • Hover Off- Cursor changes back to pointer and product card reverts to original background gradient

Mouse Down/Up Soda Image

  • Mouse Down- Soda can shakes when mouse down
  • Mouse Up- The user is taken to the soda flavor product page

Hover On/Off Product Card

  • Hover On- Product card has a drop shadow when user hover on the product card
  • Hover Off- The drop shadow is removed when user hovers off the product card
