Case Study: Poppi Soda Product Card

The Overview

Poppi Soda, a fast-growing health beverage brand, wanted their online product cards to be as fun and engaging as their drinks. The original cards were simple but didn’t spark much interaction or capture the brand’s playfulness. This project was about bringing life to the card by adding microinteractions, making the shopping experience more enjoyable and true to Poppi’s branding.

Context and Challenge

Background and Description

Poppi Soda is a fast growing health-beverage brand known for bringing back soda with gut-health benefits. The project aimed to redesign the product cards on their e-commerce site to better reflect the brand’s playful personality and engage with their customers more on their site. The existing design was functional but lacked interactivity creating an opportunity to improve the product cards to capture the spirit of the brand.

  • Timeline: 10 weeks
  • Budget: Zero Dollars

Purpose: The primary goal of the redesign was to create a more engaging shopping experience with their online site. By enhancing the product cards, Poppi Soda aimed to increase user interaction.

The Problem

Currently, the Poppi Soda product cards lack interactivity. The design of the card is more static which led to low engagement.

Goals & Objectives

This solo project focused on making Poppi Soda’s product card more interactive and engaging. By adding playful microinteractions that reflect the brand’s vibrant, health-conscious personality, the goal to increase user interaction can be accomplished.

The metrics that will define if this project is successful are through the following metrics and tangible goals:

  • Increased Engagement: Boost user interactions with the product card by adding microinteractions that are triggered by clicks and hovers.
  • Improved User Experience: Create a smoother navigation and a more intuitive design.
  • Responsive Size: Achieve seamless functionality ensuring a consistent experience on screens of all sizes.

Process and Insight

Inspiration

Before starting the design, I made sure to understand Poppi Soda’s brand and audience. With its vibrant, health-conscious branding, I knew the product cards had to match that aesthetic. I looked at what makes soda so fun like the bubbles and fizz and thought about how I could bring that same feeling to the digital experience.

Target Audience

Poppi Soda’s target audience is made up of people who care about their health and love having a fun drink. They’re looking for beverages that not only taste good but also support their wellness, and they enjoy fun, eye-catching packaging.

Prototyping

Next, I created a prototype in Figma to plan out the microinteractions. This was a helpful step in experimenting with effects like the bubble animations and shake interactions before jumping into the actual build process.

Development

Once the design was finalized, I moved into building out the product cards. Using HTML, CSS, and JavaScript, I brought the microinteractions to life adding animations like rising bubbles and the shake effect. I also made sure the cards were responsive, ensuring a smooth experience on both desktop and mobile devices.

The Solution

To make the Poppi Soda product cards more interactive, I wanted to capture the fun, bubbly aspect of soda itself. I thought about the little details that make soda fun like the bubbles that rise when you open a can or the way it shakes when it’s been stirred up. I brought these elements into the product cards when designing microinteractions, giving the design a more realistic twist. For example, I added floating bubbles to mimic the carbonation when clicking to buy the soda and a shaking effect when the user holds down on the big soda image, like the fizz you get from opening a soda can. These interactive features tackled the goal of interactive features that match Poppi's playful brand vibe. By adding these microinteractions, the product cards became more interactive that encouraged users to interact more with the brand through their site. The end result was a more seamless and interactive shopping experience that reflect Poppi’s Soda brand.

The Results

The project successfully achieved its primary goal of enhancing interactivity and user experience with Poppi Soda’s product cards. By introducing playful microinteractions that represent soda , the redesign was able to allow customers to that Poppi’s fun brand to the next level.

  • Increased Engagement: Hover and click interactions were enjoyed when testing the features with potential customers.
  • Improved User Experience: Feedback from user testing showed a more interactive experience, with users noting the cards were more fun to use.
  • Responsive Size: The product cards performed seamlessly across devices allowing a consistent experience for users on both desktop and mobile platforms.

Lesson Learned

  • Microinteractions: When breaking down the different microinteractions into Triggers, Rules, Feedback, and Loops/Modes, I struggled identifying the modes of products but after meeting with the Professor and talking through the different steps it became easy to understand the purpose of modes.
  • Clean Code: Throughout these 10-weeks, and the different steps needed to accomplish to get up to the final build I kept loosing points on assignments due to little mistakes like forgetting to change the page title or my image moving by 2 pixels. By loosing these points its made me become more aware on how to keep code and the product I am presenting clean.

Future Improvements

  • Expand Interactivity: If more time was given for this project, I would want to add additional features & layers of interactivity, such as buy now button which could further streamline the shopping process.

This project provided valuable insights into balancing creativity with usability while designing for engagement. The result is a dynamic product card design that aligns with Poppi Soda’s playful, health-focused brand identity.
